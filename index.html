Quality & Refurbishment Process

The difference
is the rebuild.

Every Berrelli unit goes through an exhaustive multi-point overhaul before it ever enters an operating room. Here is our exact 4-stage process.

01

Inspect and log

Frame integrity, boot condition, clamp wear and gas spring resistance are measured and documented against factory tolerances.

FrameBootClampGas spring
02

Strip and rebuild

The entire unit is completely disassembled. Any worn internal parts are replaced with OEM hardware and the pneumatic mechanism is precision-calibrated.

OEM HardwarePneumatic overhaul
03

Cycle test

Each assembled pair runs the full 120° arc of travel under simulated operating loads. Mechanism must lock firmly with zero drift or medial drop.

120° travel testZero drift
04

Clean, pad, ship

Units receive surgical-grade sanitization, are fitted with brand-new pressure-relieving pads, and are safely crated for rapid dispatch.

Medical sanitizationNew padsReady to use

About Berrelli

One product.
Done properly.

We do not broker surplus medical lots or retail third-party disposables. We specialize solely in surgical leg holders, rebuilding them to strict operating-room reliability.

Rebuilt by technicians, not middlemen.

Founded to solve the persistent shortages and astronomical costs of brand-new surgical positioning equipment, Berrelli delivers OEM-grade refurbished lift-assist stirrups at half the cost of new equipment—with equal or superior warranty protection.

Every unit that leaves our facility has been stripped, fitted with fresh pneumatic springs, cycle-tested across its entire 120° range of motion, and certified safe for surgical service.
